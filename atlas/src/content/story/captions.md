The captions of the story's figures. Everything above the first heading is
this note to you and never appears on the page.

HOW IT WORKS. One block per figure. The heading is the figure's number —
the same number you write in the text, as `[FIGURE 05: Press conference]` —
and everything under it, until the next heading, is that figure's caption.
Write as much as you like: blank lines make new paragraphs.

A figure whose images the reader switches with the arrow takes one block per
image: `## 2a` is the first image, `## 2b` the second. Write only `## 2` and
both images share that caption.

You can use `[the words](https://example.com)` for a link, `*italic*` and
`**bold**`. Nothing else — no HTML, ever; the page adds «Figure NN _ » in
front of the caption itself.

The number in the heading is YOUR number, the one in the text. The page
prints it seventeen higher, because the eighteen images of the opening grid
are figures 1 to 18: your `[FIGURE 02]` prints as «Figure 19», your
`[FIGURE 13]` as «Figure 30». Each heading below says which is which.

Delete a block and that figure falls back to the short name inside its
marker in the text.

## 6 — prints as Figure 23

Maps showing information from the urban plans drafted before and after the 2021
fires for the municipality of Mantoudi–Limni–Agia Anna. The map on the left shows key
zones from the General Urban Plans for the municipal units of Limni, Kireas and Nileas that
predated the fires of 2021. The map on the right shows the corresponding zones as they are
organised in the Special Urban Plan, which was donated to the municipality via the
reconstruction programme. The information presented is not exhaustive of the content
included in the official documents (see General Urban Plan for Limni, General Urban Plan for
Kireas, and General Urban Plan for Nileas). The selected information highlights the shift in
the orientation of the new Special Urban Plan compared with those that preceded it,
particularly regarding the expansion of areas designated for touristic development, the
permitted levels of industrial activity, and the protection of agricultural land.
Note: The apparent continuity between the two maps in the designation of the Mantoudi
industrial area conceals a long history of local opposition to the Type A business park. The
site is operated by TERNA MAG, a GEK TERNA subsidiary that extracts and processes
magnesite. It was designated Type A, the category permitting the highest nuisance industrial
uses, following a ministerial decision issued at the company’s request to accommodatea
proposed natural gas power plant. The classification was incorporated into the spatial
planning of the area da recommendation by the regional authority’s consultants for Type B,
which would have allowed mining to continue while excluding heavy industry. Residents
opposed the Type A designation for more than a decade and challenged it before the
Council of State. The municipal council repeatedly voted for Type B, and even the ministry’s
own review recommended examining a downgrade. The Special Urban Plan nevertheless
retains the Type A designation without revisiting this contested history. See
Chatzikonstantinou and Apostolopoulou, [“Disaster Capitalism and the Political Ecology of
Wildfire Recovery in North Evia, Greece”](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0016718526000059?via%3Dihub), Geoforum 170 (2026); Nelly Psarrou,
[“ΕΠΠΑνάσταση για την ΤΕΡΝΑ στο Μαντούδι”](https://www.efsyn.gr/periballon/1751_eppanastasi-gia-tin-terna-sto-mantoydi/), Εφημερίδα των Συντακτών, 30 October 2014;
and Psarrou, [“Σκάνδαλο στη Βόρεια Εύβοια: Μεγάλα τουριστικά συγκροτήματα και
ανεμογεννήτριες στα καμένα”](https://www.imerodromos.gr/skandalo-sti-voreia-eyvoia-megala-toyristika-sygkrotimata-kai-anemogennitries-sta-kamena/), Ημεροδρόμος, 24 October 2024.
